Demo terms

These terms cover the SALUS hackathon prototype. The demo is not a commercial service and is provided as is.

Prototype only

The demo is for evaluation and storytelling. It has no service level, no uptime guarantee, and no support commitment. Features and data may change or reset between sessions.

Synthetic data only

Every record in the demo is synthetic, including the customer file, the canary rows, and the simulated events. Do not upload real personal data, customer lists, or confidential production files to the public demo.

No legal or compliance advice

SALUS shows similarity evidence and an explainable risk score. It does not provide legal advice, a compliance certification, or proof of intent. A match is a lead, not proof. The risk weights are demo policy, not validated standards.

Availability

The demo runs on free infrastructure. The API can sleep, cold start, or reset. Keep a local run and a recorded backup for any live presentation.
